# Design

The visual system of MIDB, as built. Tokens and shared classes live in `src/app.css`; the rules agents follow are in `.claude/rules/style-standards.md` and `.claude/rules/code-standards.md` (Components).

## Idea

A quiet data console on the project's violet palette. Layout, type and components follow a pinned style reference (Podscan.fm, supplied by the maintainer); colour stays MIDB's own. The data is the hero: verdicts, sources and their values carry the colour, everything else stays neutral.

## Colour

Restrained: tinted neutrals, one violet for actions, five violet shades for sources. A good value stays violet; colour leaves the palette only to warn (copper for caution, raspberry for harm). Every value is defined once with `light-dark()`, so both themes come from the same token.

## Type

- **Display:** Inter Tight 700 (`.display`, `--font-display`), self-hosted from `static/fonts` (600 and 700 in the latin, latin-ext and vietnamese subsets, `font-display: swap`, the latin files preloaded). Hero 60px at line-height 1; page titles 30–60px; section and card headings 18–24px at 600.
- **Text:** the system UI stack (`--font-body`) at 16px / 1.5, tracked tight (-0.02em) so it reads as data. 14px for labels and buttons, 12px for captions.
- **Mono:** the system mono stack (`--font-mono`), only for year tiles.
- Field names are sentence case, never uppercase. Numbers are tabular.

## Shapes and elevation

- Radius: 8px buttons, 12px cards and inputs, 9999px pills, badges and the logo.
- Hairline borders do the separation. `--shadow-subtle` under the sticky header, `--shadow-md` for dropdowns and the poster, `--shadow-float` for one floating surface (the sign-in card), `--shadow-brand` on the brand button's hover.

## Components

- **Header:** sticky, 64px, `--surface-raised` with a hairline. The logo is "MIDB" in an ink pill; "Sign in" is an ink pill button at the right edge.
- **Card** (`.card`): the one container. Metric sections are `<details>` cards with a source icon disk, an 18px title, a status `Badge` and a chevron.
- **Section footer:** under a metric section's body, the source link with its "Updated <date>" (`.label`, `tabular-nums`, a `·` between) as one item, then Methodology as its own. The date shows only beside a source link and only when a sync is recorded; the group wraps as a unit, so the date never pairs with Methodology.
- **Badge** (`ui/badge.svelte`): pill with a tone from `tones.ts`: violet (`pos`, `brand`), copper (`mid`), raspberry (`neg`), grey (`neutral`). The tones mirror `verdict.ts`, so a badge and the verdict above it never disagree.
- **Buttons** (`.btn`): brand for the one main action per view, ink for a secondary filled action, ghost beside a filled one. A disabled button is the same shape at 55% opacity with a not-allowed cursor. A button whose action is running (`aria-busy="true"`) keeps its full colour and its width: its label is hidden under a 16px ring spinner in the label's colour, the cursor is `progress`, and the other buttons of that action wait disabled. It stays enabled, so it keeps the keyboard focus, and a second press does nothing (`+layout.svelte`); under reduced motion the ring stands still. On a coarse pointer (`(pointer: coarse)`) every button is 44px tall, the pill included.
- **Input** (`.input`): a 44px text field, 12px radius, `--border-strong` hairline that darkens on hover and turns brand with a soft ring on focus; the colour change is gated on `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Toast** (`layout/toaster.svelte`, user instruction, 2026-11-13): every action the reader takes ends in one: a success that says what was done (a comment posted, edited or deleted, a username or avatar changed, a report saved, a moderation action, signing in or out), a warning (a comment held or rejected, a report that does not count yet, a save that changed nothing) or a failure that names what failed. Votes and sorting show their result in place and raise none. Toasts stack at the foot of the viewport, bottom right in a 24rem column (full width less the gutters on a phone), errors above the rest and each group newest last, with a limit (`toasts.svelte.ts`): a `--surface-raised` plate with a 12px radius with a hairline and `--shadow-md`, a status icon in the tone's colour (`--success`, `--warn`, `--danger`), the text in `--ink` and a Close icon button. A success or warning closes after a reading time (`toastDuration`), held while pointed at or focused; an error stays until it is closed. Escape closes the newest.
- **Notice** (`ui/notice.svelte`): an inline message in a 12px-radius plate, `--danger-soft` with `--danger` text for an error, `--success-soft` with `--success` for a confirmation and `--warn-soft` with `--warn` for a warning that needs no action from the reader. It is for what holds until the page changes, beside what it describes: a field's error, a list that did not load (with Try again), and a standing state (a mute, a ban, the moderators' stale-sync line, no passkey support).
- **Plate** (`.plate`): a `--surface-sunken` block, 12px radius, 16px padding, under the row it acts on. The delete confirmation, the report form and a moderator's action panel use it; it is never nested in another plate and never holds a card.
- **Verdict:** a soft-tone callout with the axis, the tier in its tone (24px Inter Tight), a two-line summary and a disclosure listing the signals. Fixed height so the DDD stream never shifts the page.
- **Stat line:** source icon + label + value, used for the title page's source index. A film adds a Letterboxd item (`ri-star-fill`, `--src-letterboxd`) in a fixed slot: "Loading…", then "3.9/5" in place with a small `ri-external-link-line` beside it, linking to the film on Letterboxd in a new tab. "No data" means Letterboxd has no rating; "Not available" (muted, no link) means the lookup failed. Without an API key the slot is not rendered. Four columns from 900px, two from 560px.
- **Highlight** (`.highlight` + `.tone-*`): a term in its colour over a soft highlighter stroke. Status tones (`pos`, `mid`, `neg`, `neutral`) for values, source tones (`bechdel`, `um`, `ddd`, `tmdb`) for source names. Used in sentences on the landing; badges stay the form for values in data views.
- **Chip** (`.chip`): a 30px hairline pill for secondary links (Methodology, a source's domain) and the hero's film pager.
- **Comments** (`comments/`): the last section card on a title page, a header-only `CollapsibleSection` with no icon disk.
  - A composer (a `.input` grown to three rows with its name as the placeholder, the brand `Comment` button, and a `tabular-nums` counter that shows only from 80% of the length limit), a sort toggle (two `.chip`s; the pressed one takes a `--brand` border and text), then threads separated by hairlines. While the check runs, the field is read-only, the button shows the busy state and a polite status line under the composer says "Checking the comment…"; the outcome is a toast (success for a posted comment, warn for one held or rejected). Text the server refuses (too long) is a `Notice` under the field, in the line's slot, which keeps its height (one line, three on a phone) from the check until the next keystroke. The list sits in a slot with a reserved height and `aria-busy`, with the pulsing three-row skeleton, so the section never shifts when it fills.
  - A comment: one header row (a 28px avatar, the display name at 600 in `--ink`, a brand `Badge` for a moderator or administrator, the date and "Edited" in `--ink-muted`, and the "More actions" menu at the row's end), then the text, then one action row: the votes, Reply and Copy link (`ri-link`, which puts the comment's address on the clipboard and confirms with a toast). The name is a link that underlines only on hover; the date is plain text. The menu (`comments/commentMenu`, a 32px `ri-more-fill` icon button named "More actions on the comment by <name>", 44px on a coarse pointer) opens a `--surface-raised` list with a hairline, 12px radius and `--shadow-md`, holding the less frequent actions: Edit and Delete on the reader's own comment, Report on another's. Escape or a click outside closes it. Delete and Report open their plate with the focus on its sentence or first reason; Cancel returns the focus to the menu button (to Reply for a reply). Replies are one level, indented under a 1px `--border` rule that sits under the avatar, with "in reply to @name" in `.label` for a reply to a reply. A removed comment with replies is one muted line.
  - **Quiet action** (`comments/commentAction`): 14px 500 `--ink-muted` text with no fill until hover (`--surface-sunken`), 32px tall and 44px on a coarse pointer. Votes are square icon actions (`ri-arrow-up-line`, `ri-arrow-down-line`) around a `tabular-nums` score that reserves 4ch (a three-digit or negative score moves nothing); the reader's own vote is `--brand` on a 14% brand tint, set through `aria-pressed`; a press tints the action `--surface-sunken` on touch too, and every action is disabled until the reader's state has loaded or while the account is muted, and the votes stay disabled until a new account's votes open (the group's `title` and a screen-reader line read "Votes open on <date>."). A reader who cannot vote sees "Score n" in muted text and no vote buttons: on their own comment always, and signed out only when the score is not 0.
  - **Linked comment.** A link to a comment (`#comment-<id>`) opens the section and scrolls to the comment, focuses it and washes it with `--brand-wash` (the fill plus a 4px spread of the same colour, so nothing shifts) and a 1px `--brand` outline, so the mark does not rest on colour alone; the mark stays until the reader presses a key or the pointer, or the hash changes, and fades over the shared `0.15s ease` (no animation under `prefers-reduced-motion`). A comment on a page the list has not loaded is pinned above the list in a hairline-bottomed block headed by a `.label` "Linked comment", with a `--spacing-md` gap below the hairline; the first draw waits for it behind the list skeleton, and a later link keeps the drawn list and shows the block's place (one skeleton row) while its thread loads. A date is never a link (user instruction, 2026-11-14). Outside the title page, a round icon `.chip` (`comments/commentLink`, `ri-link`, named "View comment") links to the comment on its title page: right after the date in a moderation item, a recent action, a profile comment and the owner's history row, and beside Review in the table's sticky column.
  - The author's own unpublished comments carry a status `Badge` from `commentStatusTone` (copper "Awaiting review", raspberry "Rejected", with the reason leading the `.label` line, then who sees it).
  - Every failed write, and a further page of comments that did not load, is a toast naming what failed; a first page or the reader's own state that did not load is a `Notice` in its place, with Try again.
- **MIDB community reports** (`movies/metrics/communityReportsSection`): a metric section card after Unconsenting Media, closed by default, with a total in its badge. The Account data card after it is a sentence saying what the file holds, a muted line saying what it leaves out and one `.btn--ink` "Download data", which shows the busy state while the file is fetched and ends in a toast (a 429 says the limit was reached); the card is complete on the server and loads nothing. The Delete account card ends the page, set a step further from Account data than the other cards are from each other: a plain `.card` (no danger colour) with a sentence saying deletion is immediate and final, a "Removed:" group of three bulleted lines (the email, sign-in methods and sessions; the username and avatar, with the retired-name wait; comments no other account has replied below, community reports, votes and reports of comments), a "Kept:" group of one (a comment another account replied to, directly or further down, as a placeholder), and a `.btn--ghost` "Delete account". The button opens a `settingsPlate` under it with a link to Download data, a labelled username field (trimmed and lowercased as typed, `autocomplete="off"`) over a two-line reserved error slot, `.btn--ink` "Delete account permanently" and `.btn--ghost` "Keep the account"; focus moves into the plate and back to the trigger. A session older than the limit, from the load or from the endpoint's answer, and the only administrator, from the answer, replace the form with a `warn` `Notice` that takes the focus; the stale one offers "Sign out and sign in again", which returns to the card, and the administrator one says another administrator has to exist first and drops the download line. Success clears the session, goes to `/` and toasts.

## Motion

One authored moment: the landing hero's sentence change (a 0.5s fade with a slight rise and blur, timed by the active chip's fill). Otherwise `0.15s ease` on colour, border and chevron changes, a 2px brand navigation bar, a pulsing skeleton while Does the Dog Die streams, a 0.15s fade as a toast appears and the busy button's turning ring. All gated on `prefers-reduced-motion`. No other entrances, no lifts or glows.

## Browser surfaces

Focus rings (2px brand outline), selection (brand at 24%), caret (brand), scrollbars (`--border-strong`), and `select` (8px radius) are themed from the tokens in the base layer.

## Avoid

Green or red for "good" and "bad" (the status tokens carry that), kickers above headings, uppercase tracked labels, coloured side borders over 1px, gradients, hover lifts and glows, pulsing badges, arrow glyphs as icons, decorative imagery, hues outside the violet shades and status tokens, and placeholders for features that do not exist.
